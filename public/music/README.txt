PLACE YOUR SONG HERE — GITHUB UPLOAD INSTRUCTIONS

1. Rename your authorized audio file exactly: our-song.mp3
2. In GitHub, open public/music/ in your repository.
3. Click Add file → Upload files. Select our-song.mp3 and commit.
4. Railway redeploys automatically if autodeploy is enabled.
5. Visit https://YOUR-RAILWAY-DOMAIN/music/our-song.mp3 to confirm audio loads.
6. On the birthday site, tap the record to play/pause. It also starts when the letter is opened.

For M4A instead: upload our-song.m4a and change musicUrl in public/config.js
to "/music/our-song.m4a".

Only host audio that you have permission to share over a public website.
